Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026.

Beneath the Throne is a browser game. It does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Save files, language, supporter cosmetics, and checkout-link settings are stored in your browser (localStorage). A service worker may cache game files so the playtest can reload offline.

What we do not collect

This build does not run an account server, analytics suite, or crash reporter. We do not receive your save data unless you choose to send it.

Payments

Playtest checkout can grant cosmetics on this device without a card. If you later paste Stripe Payment Links, Stripe processes the payment under Stripe’s privacy policy. In an App Store or Google Play wrapper, store billing rules apply instead of Stripe for digital goods.

Ads

The Court Crier is a local stand-in for an optional rewarded ad. No third-party ad network is wired in this build.

Contact

Questions: use the support channel you used to get this build.
