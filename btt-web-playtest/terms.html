Saves

Progress lives in your browser. Clearing site data, switching devices, or a failed update can lose saves. Make a slot save before a major update when you can.

Purchases

Paid offers in the Court Ledger are cosmetics, titles, extra save slots, and optional ad removal. They do not buy combat power. Playtest “Pay” grants locally and may not charge a card until real checkout links are configured.
