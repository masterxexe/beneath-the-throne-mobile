# The Obsidian Library

_Knowledge is power, but some secrets were never meant to be found. The pages whisper, and the shadows listen..._

Centuries ago, a conclave of archmages sealed away forbidden knowledge in a library carved from volcanic obsidian. Now the wards have weakened, and those seeking dangerous power have begun to breach its depths.

 

## RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

d4 | Details
---|--------
 1 | A **living book** flaps through the air, pages rustling menacingly
 2 | A **shadow** slithers across walls, watching intruders
 3 | 1d4 **cultists** searching for a specific tome
 4 | A **bound demon** appears, offering knowledge for freedom 
 
***Whispers of Madness.*** Any character who reads from an unmarked tome must save versus spells or suffer vivid hallucinations for 1d4 hours.

***Animated Guardians.*** The library's **animated armors** can pass through obsidian walls as if they were mist.

**1. Fallen Entrance**. A collapsed archway leads into a chamber with cracked obsidian tiles. Three animated armors stand frozen like statues unless disturbed.
- A ghostly librarian materializes near the entrance, offering to "assist worthy scholars" before vanishing. It reappears in Area 7.

**2. Reading Room**. Obsidian tables and benches fill this chamber. A ghoul in tattered scholarly robes hunches over ancient scrolls, muttering calculations.
- The ghoul was once a mathematician who sought immortality through numbers. If approached peacefully, it will trade a Scroll of Protection for fresh blood (1 HP from each party member).
- Carved into one table is a map showing Areas 3-5.

**3. Silent Stacks**. Towering bookshelves filled with countless tomes. A poltergeist hurls books at intruders.
- The ceiling is covered in luminous magical script that shifts and changes. Reading it requires a save versus spells or the reader is stunned for 1d4 rounds.
- A hidden door in the northeast corner leads to Area 8, revealed only when a specific book titled "Doorways Unseen" is removed from its shelf.

**4. Archivists' Quarters**. Spartan living quarters with simple beds and desks. 2d4 skeletons in ragged robes work endlessly, cataloging and organizing scrolls.
- A locked chest beneath one bed contains 240 gp and a silver quill worth 100 gp that never runs out of ink.

**5. Binding Chamber**. A large circular room with a runic circle on the floor. Bookbinding tools are arranged neatly on tables around the edges.
- A demon is partially bound within the circle. It offers knowledge (one answer to any question) in exchange for freedom.
- The binding is weakening; any loud noise or magical disturbance has a 3:6 chance of freeing the demon (treat as barbed devil).

**6. Whispering Gallery**. A long, curved corridor where whispers echo from nowhere. Each character must save versus spells or be compelled to reveal their deepest secret aloud.
- The whispers grow louder with each secret revealed, potentially alerting creatures in Areas 7 and 9.

**7. Forbidden Archives**. A large chamber with black iron lockboxes containing the most dangerous texts. The ghostly librarian from Area 1 floats here, now clearly insane.
- The librarian (specter) attacks anyone touching the lockboxes.
- One box contains The Codex of Stolen Thoughts worth 2,000 gp to the right buyer, but it slowly drains Intelligence from its reader.

**8. Hidden Study**. A small room accessible only through the secret door in Area 3. An ornate desk holds a journal detailing the library's creation.
- A dagger of venom is concealed in a hidden compartment in the desk.
- The study contains a one-way viewing portal to Area 10, appearing as a large mirror.

**9. The Burning Room**. A chamber where forbidden books are destroyed. Magical flames burn eternally in a large brazier.
- The flames can destroy cursed items but also attract a fire elemental that emerges if anything valuable is thrown in.
- Among the partially burned texts is a fragment of _The Necromancer's Path_ worth 350 gp.

**10. The Mind's Eye**. A spherical chamber at the heart of the library. Floating in the center is a large crystal orb containing the consciousness of the library's founder.
- The founder (lich with diminished powers) has been driven mad by isolation. It initially appears as a benevolent guide but reveals its true nature when questioned about leaving the library.
- The crystal orb acts as both prison and power source. Destroying it frees the lich but also collapses the library in 1d4 turns.
- A shelf of immaculately preserved tomes contains spells and knowledge worth 3,000 gp total, but each book weighs 20 lbs and radiates evil.

# Valley of the Red Scorpion

_The sands hide a forgotten evil, buried but not destroyed. Those who seek ancient treasures may instead awaken the horrors imprisoned within the desert's depths._

A generation ago, the Overlord dispatched an expedition to excavate mysterious ruins in a remote desert canyon. None returned. Now, a cult of lizardfolk worships the malevolent force that was unearthed below the shifting sands.

 

## RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

 d4 | Details
----|:-------
 1 | A **scarab swarm** erupts suddenly from the sand
 2 | A **giant scorpion** drags a fresh kill toward Area 3
 3 | 2d4 **lizardfolk** gathering offerings for their dark patron
 4 | An **efreet** appears, seeking to bargain with the high priest in Area 9

***Idol of Raz Kha'la***. This solid silver idol contains a bound **horned devil**. It can only be safely handled after performing the ritual detailed in the book found in Area 9. Otherwise, touching it releases the fiend.

***Desert Lizardfolk***. These variants can climb a near distance rather than swim and have adapted to the harsh desert environment.

**1. Storage Chamber**. Crates and woven baskets filled with excavation equipment line the walls. Records detail an ill-fated expedition. A **cobra** lurks among the supplies, striking if disturbed.
- The final entry in a dust-covered journal speaks of a "marble chamber deep below" and "strange silver reflections."
- A narrow **bridge** spans from here to Area 4, passing above Area 6. Loud noises disturb slumbering bats below; disturbed bats require a DC 13 DEX save or characters fall 30' into Area 6.

**2. Passage of the Fallen**. 2d4 human **skeletons** lie scattered throughout this corridor, the remains of miners who fled in terror.
- Any discussion of dark magic, murder, or betrayal causes these remains to animate as hostile undead.
- Faded bloodstains on the walls form patterns that resemble arcane symbols when viewed in torchlight.

**3. Scorpion's Lair**. A massive **giant scorpion** (26 HP) has made its nest in this cavern. Partially devoured lizardfolk corpses lie scattered around its territory.
- One deceased lizardfolk wears a gold bangle worth 150 gp around its arm.
- Scorpion eggs nestle in a crevice; if destroyed, the adult becomes frenzied, gaining an extra attack each round.

**4. Mining Network**. A labyrinth of excavated tunnels and natural caverns extends throughout this area, many ending in collapsed dead ends.
- Rickety wooden **stairs** at the southern end descend to the lower level. They are magically warded; approaching from the north causes an illusory giant lizard to appear, blocking passage.
- A crushed lizardfolk lies beneath a cave-in, still clutching a chunk of gold ore worth 100 gp.

**5. Prisoner's Hold**. A dwarf named Tannek is chained to the wall with rusted manacles. Weeks of captivity have left him mad with fear and desperation.
- He begs for release but becomes incoherent when questioned about what lies deeper in the complex.
- **Tracks** in the dust deliberately avoid the northern passage leading to Area 3.

**6. Lower Crevasse**. A wooden platform attached to a winch and cable system provides access to the chamber 30 feet below (Area 9).
- Operating the mechanism has a 2:6 chance of disturbing sleeping bats, which fly out of the shaft and alert the **lizardfolk** in Area 7.
- Ancient warning glyphs carved into the rock walls have been deliberately defaced.

**7. Lizardfolk Encampment**. 2d4 **lizardfolk** cultists maintain a crude camp in this chamber, resting between ritual observances.
- A mated pair are more loyal to each other than to their priest in Area 9 and might be persuaded to flee rather than fight.
- The group possesses jewelry crafted from roc bone and feathers worth 100 gp collectively.

**8. Treasure Chamber**. Accessible only through a hole in the ceiling of Area 7. A drowsy **roper** guards substantial treasures taken from previous victims.
- The creature can be temporarily placated with fresh bat meat from the shaft.
- Among the treasures are 340 gp in various coins and a Scarab of Protection stolen from the dwarf in Area 5.

**9. Ritual Chamber**. A lizardfolk priest wearing an elaborate gold and blue enamel headdress performs ceremonial dances before a pair of imposing brass doors.
- The priest (use **kobold sorcerer** stats but can summon a **scarab swarm** instead of spiders) calls for reinforcements from Area 7 if threatened.
- His ornate headdress is worth 300 gp to collectors.
- A **ritual book** written in ancient hieroglyphics shows a figure in ceremonial attire controlling a fiendish entity (related to the *Idol of Raz Kha'la*). The tome would fetch 1000 gp from the right buyer.

**10. Inner Sanctum**. A magnificent chamber constructed of marble pillars and alabaster walls. A silver idol rests upon a central pedestal, while an open sarcophagus sits to the north.
- A bottomless **pit** in the floor descends into infernal depths (treat as a 200-foot fall).
- The **silver idol** radiates palpable malevolence to anyone who approaches within close range.
- Within the open sarcophagus lies a **mummy** dressed in white priestly vestments. If the fiend is released from the idol, the mummy animates and attacks the horned devil.

# Frostfang Catacombs

_The frozen dead stir in their tombs. Ancient oaths broken, an evil awakens within the ice-carved halls of fallen heroes._

Deep in the Frostspire Mountains, the Frostfang Catacombs house the remains of a forgotten kingdom's greatest warriors. A recent earthquake has disturbed more than just the ice - something ancient has awakened.

 

## RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

 d4 | Details
----|:-------
 1 | A **frost skeleton** patrolling with an ancient, rusted weapon
 2 | An **ice mephit** playing malicious pranks on the living
 3 | 1d6 **winter wolves** hunting together in the frigid passages
 4 | The ghostly vision of an ancient battle plays out before fading away

***Bitter Cold***. Characters must make a CON save every 4 hours or gain one level of exhaustion unless properly equipped for extreme cold.

***Frozen Warriors***. The **frost skeletons** shatter when reduced to 0 HP, dealing 1d4 cold damage to all creatures within near distance.

**1. Entrance Hall**. Ice-slick steps descend into a grand entrance chamber where frozen banners hang from the ceiling. Two **frost skeletons** guard an ornate stone door.
- The **floor** is treacherously slippery; any character running or fighting must make a DC 13 DEX save or fall prone.
- Ancient runes circle the doorway, warning in a forgotten tongue about disturbing the sleeping warriors.

**2. Hall of Heroes**. Stone sarcophagi line the walls of this massive chamber, each carved with the likeness of a warrior. Ice crystals grow from the ceiling.
- If any sarcophagus is opened, 1d4 **frost skeletons** rise from nearby tombs.
- A **frozen body** of a recent explorer clutches a map of the catacombs (shows Areas 3-6) and a potion of cold resistance.

**3. Armory**. Racks of ancient, frost-rimed weapons and armor stand in neat rows. A **winter wolf** has made its den here and protects three cubs.
- Among the mundane weapons is a **frost brand sword** still in perfect condition.
- The wolf guards a pile of bones containing 180 gp and a platinum circlet worth 250 gp.

**4. Royal Sepulcher**. A grand tomb chamber with a massive sarcophagus on a raised dais. A ghostly **wraith** in royal regalia circles the room.
- The wraith was once Queen Iselda the Unyielding, betrayed by her general. She attacks anyone resembling a soldier but may speak with others.
- Her **crown**, encrusted with ice diamonds, rests on her remains and is worth 800 gp.
- A secret passage beneath the sarcophagus leads to Area 8.

**5. Ritual Chamber**. A circular room with a frozen pool in the center. The ceiling is painted with constellations.
- The **pool** is actually a portal to the Elemental Plane of Ice. Anyone touching it must save versus spells or be pulled in (and ejected in Area 9).
- Four **ice mephits** dance around the pool and try to trick visitors into touching the water.

**6. Collapsed Treasury**. Half of this room has collapsed under the weight of ice. Frozen bodies of treasure hunters are visible beneath fallen stones.
- Searching the rubble has a 2:6 chance of causing more collapse, dealing 2d6 damage to anyone in the room.
- Hidden among the debris is a small chest containing 340 gp and a map leading to a greater treasure in distant mountains.

**7. Frozen Library**. Shelves of ice-preserved scrolls and tomes line the walls. An **ice elemental** guards this chamber against all intruders.
- The knowledge here includes lost ice magic and the true history of the kingdom's fall, worth 600 gp to scholars.
- A **frozen wizard** stands perfectly preserved in a block of ice. If thawed, he awakens as a **revenant** seeking vengeance against a bloodline that may include party members.

**8. Secret Vault**. A small chamber accessible only through the passage in Area 4. Contains treasures of the royal family.
- A **trapped chest** releases a freezing cloud when opened improperly (DC 15 DEX save or take 3d6 cold damage).
- The chest contains 450 gp, a **ring of warmth**, and a map to the king's hidden summer palace.

**9. The Deep Shrine**. A vast cathedral-like space deep below the catacombs. A gigantic creature is partially visible, embedded in the far wall of ice.
- The **frost titan** (treat as **frost giant** with double HP) is slowly breaking free of the ice. If the party lingers or makes loud noises, it breaks free and attacks.
- The titan wears an amulet of protection that grants resistance to cold damage.
- Ancient offerings worth 700 gp in gold and platinum coins are scattered at its feet.

**10. Heart of Winter**. The source of the catacombs' awakening: a pulsing blue crystal the size of a human head, floating above an altar of black ice.
- The **crystal** is an artifact called the Winter's Heart. Taking it stops the awakening of the dead but summons an **ice devil** to retrieve it.
- The chamber contains the perfectly preserved body of General Vorgath, the betrayer of Queen Iselda, wearing armor worth 900 gp and bearing a **frost brand greatsword**.
- Destroying the Winter's Heart causes the catacombs to begin collapsing in 1d6 turns.

# The Sunken Temple of Ix

_The waters rise, the ancient walls creak, and the forgotten god stirs from slumber. Those who disturb Ix shall know the crushing depths._

A coastal temple dedicated to Ix, a forgotten sea deity, has been gradually reclaimed by the ocean. Local fisherfolk speak of strange lights and haunting songs emanating from the half-submerged ruins.

 

## RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

 d4 | Details
----|:-------
 1 | 1d4 **sahuagin** scouting the temple for treasures
 2 | A **water elemental** forms from the surrounding pools
 3 | 2d4 human **cultists** performing a ritual to Ix
 4 | A ghostly procession of drowned priests chanting in ancient tongues

***Rising Tides***. Every 6 hours, the water level throughout the temple rises or falls by 10 feet, potentially submerging or revealing different areas.

***Corrupted Waters***. Any fresh water brought into the temple turns brackish and undrinkable after 1 hour. Water from the temple causes hallucinations if consumed.

**1. Drowned Courtyard**. A once-grand courtyard now half-submerged in seawater. Algae-covered statues of fish-human hybrids line the perimeter.
- **Stairs** lead down into water-filled chambers below. A DC 12 Perception check reveals an air pocket in Area 3.
- A waterlogged journal on a floating piece of debris details previous explorers' discoveries in Areas 2 and 4.

**2. Hall of Offerings**. Barnacle-encrusted offering bowls line shelves along the walls. 2d4 **sahuagin** rummage through ancient treasures.
- The **ceiling** drops seawater in a rhythmic pattern. Following the pattern reveals a hidden alcove containing a **trident of fish command**.
- Among the offerings are pearls worth 200 gp and a coral figurine of Ix worth 150 gp.

**3. Air Pocket Sanctuary**. A large chamber with a trapped air pocket in the upper portion. The walls are covered in murals depicting Ix blessing worshippers with transformations.
- A **sea hag** has made this her lair, keeping a collection of trinkets from drowned sailors.
- Her **treasure** includes 230 gp, a magical conch shell that allows water breathing once per day, and an amber amulet worth 350 gp.

**4. Sacrificial Pool**. A deep, circular pool dominates this chamber. Ancient bloodstains surround the edge.
- The **pool** descends 100 feet to a hidden chamber (Area 8). A giant **octopus** lurks at 50 feet down.
- Skeletal remains of sacrifices line the bottom of the pool, some wearing jewelry worth 400 gp total.

**5. Priest's Quarters**. Partially collapsed living quarters for the temple's priests. Most furnishings have rotted away.
- A **chest** sealed with wax has protected its contents from water: ceremonial robes, a silver mask worth 180 gp, and a scroll of water breathing.
- A **trapped doorway** releases poisonous sea urchin spines when opened incorrectly (DC 14 DEX save or take 2d6 poison damage).

**6. The Proving Grounds**. A large chamber where initiates were tested. Rusty chains and hooks hang from the ceiling.
- 1d4 **cultists** and a **cult fanatic** are attempting to recreate ancient rituals here.
- The fanatic carries a **ceremonial dagger** that deals an extra 1d6 cold damage and can breathe underwater for 10 minutes after drawing blood.

**7. Ix's Oracle**. A chamber with a raised dais above water level. A **merrow** claiming to speak for Ix demands tribute from all who enter.
- The oracle knows secrets about the temple, including the passage to Area 10, but speaks only in riddles.
- Behind the dais is a hidden compartment containing 300 gp and a vial of oil that grants water breathing for 1 hour when consumed.

**8. Abyssal Chamber**. Accessible only through the depths of the pool in Area 4. The walls pulse with bioluminescent algae.
- A **sea spawn** (use **ghoul** stats with swimming) tends to egg-like pods attached to the walls.
- The eggs contain humanoid-fish hybrids; destroying them alerts the entity in Area 10.

**9. The Changing Room**. A chamber filled with large, person-sized shells. A thick green liquid pools on the floor.
- Anyone touching the liquid must save versus poison or begin a slow transformation into a fish-human hybrid (gain swimming speed but disadvantage on social interactions).
- A withered corpse wears a **ring of mind shielding** and clutches notes detailing how to reverse the transformation using the altar in Area 10.

**10. Throne of the Deep**. The temple's heart: a vast chamber with a coral throne. The ceiling is open to the sea above, with a swirling whirlpool connecting to the depths.
- On the throne sits a partially transformed priestess (**sea sorceress** - use **mage** stats with water spells). She is the last true worshipper of Ix.
- The **altar** before the throne can reverse transformations but requires a sacrifice of 500 gp worth of precious metals.
- A **portal** beneath the throne connects to Ix's realm. If disturbed, a **water elemental** emerges, followed by more each round until the portal is sealed.
- The chamber contains offerings worth 1,200 gp in total, including a **pearl of power** embedded in the throne.

# The Endless Markets

_Between worlds, where shadow meets light, the Endless Markets await. Bring coin and courage, for every bargain has its price._

The Endless Markets exist in a pocket dimension accessible only through magical doorways that appear at midnight in abandoned marketplaces. Merchants from across the planes gather to trade in goods both wondrous and terrible.

 

## RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

 d4 | Details
----|:-------
 1 | A **rakshasa** disguised as a human merchant, selling "genuine" magical items
 2 | 1d4 **quasits** stealing from unattended stalls
 3 | A **tiefling** barker loudly advertising dangerous services
 4 | A dimensional rift temporarily opens, revealing a glimpse of a distant plane

***Market Rules***. Violence against merchants or theft from stalls summons 1d4 **invisible stalkers** as market security.

***Constant Haggling***. Prices fluctuate wildly. Each hour, roll 1d6: 1-3 means prices increase by 25%, 4-6 means they decrease by 25%.

**1. Gateway Plaza**. The arrival point: a circular plaza with doors of various designs floating in midair. Colorful tents and stalls create a labyrinth of commerce.
- A **giff** (hippo-headed humanoid, use **bugbear** stats) sells maps of the market for 10 gp. The maps are mostly accurate but deliberately omit Area 8.
- Floating **lanterns** change color to indicate time until the gateway closes (green: 6+ hours, yellow: 2-6 hours, red: less than 2 hours).

**2. Curios Row**. Stalls selling trinkets, oddities, and small magical items line a narrow alley. A **kenku** merchant mimics customers' voices as they browse.
- One stall sells vials of "memories" (vivid illusions when consumed) for 50 gp each.
- A **trapped** chest appears abandoned but is actually bait; tampering with it summons the owner, a **cambion** demanding compensation.

**3. Flesh Emporium**. A disturbing tent where body modifications and transformations are sold. Jars of preserved specimens line the walls.
- The proprietor, a **medusa** wearing a veil, offers temporary or permanent transformations for appropriate prices.
- A captive **dryad** is being sold for 500 gp. She promises a reward if returned to her forest.

**4. The Menagerie**. Exotic creatures from across the planes are bought and sold in magical cages.
- A **beholder** merchant (diminished powers) sells trained guard beasts. It knows secrets about Area 10.
- One cage contains a **shadow mastiff** puppy for 300 gp that bonds with its purchaser.
- A **trapped** cage appears empty but contains an invisible **air elemental** that escapes if opened.

**5. House of Fortunes**. A tall, narrow building where divinations and prophecies are sold.
- Three **hags** (disguised as elegant ladies) offer to read futures for 100 gp. The prophecies are true but deliberately misleading.
- The back room contains a portal to Area 9, accessible only to those who've purchased a reading.

**6. Armaments Square**. A wide plaza where weapons and armor from countless worlds are displayed on racks and mannequins.
- A **fire giant** blacksmith offers weapon upgrades that add 1d4 elemental damage for 500 gp.
- A cursed **shield of missile attraction** is prominently displayed as a "bargain" at 100 gp.
- The armor stands are actually **animated armors** that prevent theft.

**7. Nexus Tavern**. A rowdy establishment where merchants and customers alike gather to drink exotic beverages.
- The bartender, a **dao** (earth genie), collects gossip as payment and knows about all areas of the market.
- A group of **duergar** mercenaries offer protection services for 50 gp per hour.
- The chairs at one table are actually mimics that attack drunken patrons.

**8. The Black Vault**. A hidden auction house for the most dangerous and illegal items, accessible only through invitation.
- An **archmage** runs the auction, selling items like prison orbs containing demons, books of forbidden knowledge, and assassins' contracts.
- The current auction item is a **sphere of annihilation** with a control talisman, starting bid 2,000 gp.
- **Guards** (use **gladiator** stats) with magical restraints patrol the perimeter.

**9. The Weave Bazaar**. A magical marketplace where spells and enchantments are bought and sold like commodities.
- Spell scrolls of levels 1-3 are available for standard prices.
- A **djinni** offers to enchant items for half the normal cost but has a 2:6 chance of adding an unpredictable side effect.
- A **grimoire** containing three random spells is chained to a pedestal. Reading it without paying 200 gp triggers a **glyph of warding**.

**10. The Proprietor's Office**. The hidden nerve center of the Endless Markets, accessible only through a door that appears differently to each viewer.
- The Proprietor (**arcanaloth** with enhanced powers) keeps ledgers of all debts and bargains made in the market.
- Magical **surveillance mirrors** show every area of the market.
- A **contract** on the desk details an upcoming "clearance" where all visitors will be trapped and sold as commodities.
- The Proprietor's **treasury** contains 5,000 gp, gems worth 2,000 gp, and a **staff of power**, but stealing from it permanently bars the thief from ever entering the Endless Markets again.
